<p><strong>Learners who Share -  Experience ITE Programme (EIP)</strong>
</p>
<p>On 30 April, our 2G1 students had the opportunity to visit ITE College
Central as part of their Experience ITE Programme (EIP). They engaged in
various hands-on activities such as flight simulation, livestream selling,
robotics, and animation. Through these interactive sessions, students gained
meaningful insights into different learning pathways and were encouraged
to explore their interests and future aspirations.</p>
