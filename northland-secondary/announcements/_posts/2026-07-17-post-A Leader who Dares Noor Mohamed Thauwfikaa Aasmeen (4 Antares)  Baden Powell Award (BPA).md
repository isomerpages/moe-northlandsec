<h4><strong>A Leader who Dares - Noor Mohamed Thauwfikaa Aasmeen (4 Antares) - Baden-Powell Award (BPA)</strong></h4>
<p>We are proud to celebrate Noor Mohamed Thauwfikaa Aasmeen (Fikaa) (4 Antares)
for attaining the Baden-Powell Award (BPA), the highest award conferred
by the Chief Commissioner upon a Girl Guide. To attain the Baden-Powell
Award (BPA), a Guide must obtain at least 70% in both the written and execution
tests, reflecting a high standard of competence, leadership and commitment.
Congratulations, Fikaa, on this remarkable achievement! We are incredibly
proud of you!</p>
