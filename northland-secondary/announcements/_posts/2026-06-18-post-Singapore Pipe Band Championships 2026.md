<p><strong>Leaders who Dare - Singapore Pipe Band Championships 2026</strong>
</p>
<p>After months of hard work, dedication and intensive practice, a total
of 10 students and 1 alumnus proudly represented the school at the Singapore
Pipe Band Championships on 3 June 2026 held at St Joesph’s Institution.</p>
<p>The students demonstrated resilience, discipline and teamwork throughout
their preparation, dedicating themselves to improving both their individual
skills and ensemble performance. Their efforts were rewarded with several
outstanding individual achievements and a strong showing in the Singapore
Pipe Band Championships.</p>
<p>We are proud to share the achievements of our students:</p>
<p>Individual Achievements</p>
<p>* Phua Rui Wen (4R) – 1st Place, D Grade Tenor Drumming Solo</p>
<p>* Cash Ng (alumnus) –2nd place in D Grade Piobaireachd Solo</p>
<p>* Chua Dian Yang Wayne (2S) – 2nd Place, Novice Piping Solo</p>
<p>* Lynn Thu Nyo (2S) – 2nd Place, Novice Tenor Drumming Solo</p>
<p>* Seah Yun Shan Christie (2C) – 3rd Place, Novice Snare Drumming Solo</p>
<p>Band Achievement</p>
<p>* Northland Secondary School Pipe Band – 4th Place in the Band Championships
(out of 8 bands who have participated)</p>
<p>This year's result is particularly meaningful as it marks the band's return
to the Band Championships after a two-year hiatus. Competing against experienced
bands, our students delivered a commendable performance and narrowly missed
a podium finish by just one point.</p>
<p>Regardless of the final rankings, every participant represented Northland
with pride, resilience and sportsmanship. We are incredibly proud of their
commitment, growth and determination throughout this journey.</p>
<p>We would like to congratulate all participants for representing the school
with pride and thank our instructors, teachers, alumni and supporters for
their invaluable guidance and encouragement.</p>
