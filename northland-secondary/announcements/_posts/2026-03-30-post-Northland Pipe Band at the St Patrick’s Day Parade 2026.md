<p><strong>Leaders who Dare - Northland Pipe Band at the St Patrick’s Day Parade 2026</strong>
</p>
<p>Our Northland Secondary School Pipe Band proudly participated in the St
Patrick’s Day Parade held in the city area on Sunday. Our Northstars had
the opportunity to participate in the parade alongside the Merlion Pipe
Band Singapore and members of the Boys’ Brigade Pipe Band from Malaysia
Penang, soaking in the vibrant atmosphere of this festive celebration together
with the Irish community in Singapore.</p>
<p>Through the weeks of preparation, the Pipe Band members demonstrated commitment,
discipline and teamwork as they worked together to present their best performance.</p>
<p>We are proud of our Northstars for their dedication and for conducting
themselves well throughout the event. Well done, Northland Pipe Band!</p>
