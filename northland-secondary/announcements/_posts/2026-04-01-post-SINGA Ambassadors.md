<p><strong>Citizens who Care- SINGA Ambassadors</strong>
</p>
<p>SINGA Ambassadors hosted an Integration Programme for our International
Students and Permanent Residents (ISPR), helping them settle into our school,
understand our education system, and feel part of the wider Singapore community
🇸🇬 From exploring the Civic District to shared conversations and experiences,
it was all about building connections, understanding, and a sense of belonging.</p>
